Questions and answers

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you a menu link and QR code for up to 10 dishes. Pro adds ordering, table QR codes, analytics, reviews and unlimited dishes.

Do guests need to install an app?

No. They scan the QR code with the phone camera and the menu opens in the browser.

Can I change prices later?

Yes, any time. The QR code never changes, only your menu updates.

Where do orders arrive?

You choose: your WhatsApp, your dashboard (with a sound alert), or both.

Your menu can be live in 2 minutes.

No card needed. Upgrade only when you grow.
