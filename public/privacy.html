Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what data MenuLoom collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Owners: email, password (stored by Firebase Authentication, never visible to us), business name, type, country, address, WhatsApp number, logo, menu items, photos, settings and payment status.
  • Guests: no account is needed. We count menu views per day, store orders you place (items, table number, time) when the restaurant uses the dashboard, and store reviews you choose to write (rating, text, optional name).
  • Technical: your language and consent choice are kept in your browser (local storage). We do not use advertising cookies.

How we use it

To run the menu, show orders and analytics to the Owner, process payments, prevent abuse, improve the Service and answer support requests.

Who processes data

Google Firebase (login and database), Cloudflare (website hosting), Cloudinary (images, if enabled), Paddle (payments, as merchant of record). They process data under their own policies. Orders sent through WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Menus, business details, reviews and feedback you publish are public by design. We may share data if the law requires it.

Retention and your rights

We keep data while your account is active. You can edit or delete menus and reviews in the dashboard, and ask us to delete your account and data by email. Depending on your country you may also have rights to access, correct or export your data.

Security

Data is stored with Google Firebase using access rules so only the Owner can read their orders and analytics. No system is 100% secure.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13.
