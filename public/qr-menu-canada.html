A practical digital menu for Canada food businesses

Help guests browse your restaurant menu from their own phones with a simple QR code. MenuLoom provides a shareable menu page for Canadian restaurants, cafes and food businesses.

Whether your business is in Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, Ottawa or Montreal, a live digital menu can make menu changes more convenient. Keep your QR code on tables or counter cards, and update the menu page when dishes or prices change. If you serve customers in multiple languages, add the translations you support to your menu.

How the QR menu works

  1. Create your restaurant menu and add dish names, descriptions, photos and prices.
  2. Publish the menu and download its QR code.
  3. Place the QR code on tables, counter cards, takeaway packaging or other customer touchpoints.
  4. Guests scan with a phone camera and view the menu in their browser.
  5. When your menu changes, update the live page; the QR code can continue pointing to it.

Useful for restaurants, cafes and food trucks

Use one digital menu link wherever customers need to see your latest dishes. A QR menu is a menu-publishing tool; it does not automatically replace your point-of-sale system, payment processor or delivery marketplace.
