How to create a QR menu

  1. Create your menu and add dish names, descriptions, photos and prices.
  2. Publish the menu and download its QR code.
  3. Print and place the QR code on tables, counter cards or takeaway packaging.
  4. Guests scan it with a phone camera and view the menu in their browser.
  5. Update the live menu when dishes, availability or prices change; the QR code can keep pointing to the same page.

Frequently asked questions

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Guests open the menu in their phone browser after scanning the QR code.

Can I update prices without printing a new QR code?

Yes. Update the live menu page; the existing QR code can continue linking to it.

Does a QR menu replace a POS or payment system?

No. A QR menu publishes menu information. It should not be represented as a replacement for a point-of-sale system, payment processor or delivery marketplace.
