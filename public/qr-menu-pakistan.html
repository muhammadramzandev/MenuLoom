How it works

  1. Sign up with Google or email and enter your restaurant name.
  2. Add your dishes with prices and photos.
  3. Download your QR code and print it for the tables.
  4. Customers scan and browse. On Pro they order on WhatsApp.

Questions owners ask

Does it work for hotels and restaurants in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad?

Yes. Any restaurant with a phone and WhatsApp can use it, in any city.

Is the QR menu really free?

Yes. The free plan gives you a menu link and a printable QR code for up to 10 dishes. Pro adds WhatsApp ordering, table QR codes, analytics and unlimited dishes.

How do customers order?

On the Pro plan, guests scan the QR code, add dishes to the cart and send the order to your WhatsApp. The order also appears in your dashboard.

Do I need to reprint the QR code when prices change?

No. The QR code always opens your live menu, so you only edit the dish or price once.

Can I show my menu in other languages?

Yes. When you add translations, guests see your menu in their own phone language: English, Urdu, Arabic, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish or French.
